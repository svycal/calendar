Add `MonthView`, a Google Calendar–style month grid. All-day and multi-day events render as bars that wrap across week rows, single-day timed events render as dot items, and days that run out of room collapse extras into a "+N more" popover. Supports click or drag to select day ranges (shown as a Google-style placeholder bar by default), a configurable week start, and fixed six-week layouts.
