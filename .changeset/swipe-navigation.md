Swipe sideways on a touch screen to show the previous or next period, like Google Calendar's mobile app, in `ResourceGridView`, `DayGridView`, and `MonthView` with `onSwipe`. The page follows the finger, revealing the neighboring period's empty grid, and turns when released past a third of its width or flung. Change the view's date in response; it holds the turned page until you do.
