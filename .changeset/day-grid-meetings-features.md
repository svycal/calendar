Add DayGridView features for replacing FullCalendar: multi-day drag selection (`multiDaySelection`, `DayGridSelectedRange.endDate` and `allDay`), background events (`backgroundEvents`, `renderBackgroundEvent`), `onSlotHover`, `formatGutterLabel` (also on ResourceGridView), `scrollKey`, and display-only events (`interactive: false`). Selections are now clipped to the time axis.
